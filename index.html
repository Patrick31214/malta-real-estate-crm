How to start the CRM server
1
Open Windows Explorer — click the yellow folder icon on your taskbar. Do NOT use a web browser for this step.
2
Navigate to your CRM folder:
C:\Users\USER\malta-crm\malta-real-estate-crm
3
Find start-windows.bat and double-click it.
A black window opens — this is correct. Wait about 20 seconds.
Keep that black window open while using the CRM.
4
Come back to this page and click ↺ Check again, then click OPEN THE CRM.
⚠ Important: start-windows.bat is a program, not a website. You cannot open it in a browser. Always double-click it in Windows Explorer (the yellow folder), not in Edge or Chrome. Closing the black window will stop the CRM server.
